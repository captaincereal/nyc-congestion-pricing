# Methodology

Frozen definitions are in `docs/project_brief.md`. This document is the detailed
statistical specification. Changes to a frozen item must be justified here with
a dated note.

## Treatment

CRZ tolling began on **2025-01-05** (`TREATMENT_DATE`). Treated units are DOT
speed links physically inside the Congestion Relief Zone (Manhattan ≤ 60th St,
excluding the exempt highways). Control units are comparable links outside the
zone, selected on pre-treatment behaviour (Phase 5). Boundary links are held
out of the control group and modelled separately for spillover.

## Primary outcome

Hourly **median** link speed in mph, aggregated from the sub-hourly feed to
`link_id` × hour. Median (not mean) to limit the influence of sparse extreme
readings within an hour. Mean speed and observation count are carried as
secondary / diagnostic series.

## Design 1 — Descriptive (Phase 6)

- Treated vs control median speed over time; treated−control difference over
  time; pre-treatment trends.
- Hourly profiles; weekday vs weekend; peak vs off-peak.
- Observation counts and link coverage over time.
- 2025-01-05 marked on every time-series plot. No causal claims.

## Design 2 — Difference-in-differences (Phase 7)

Two-way fixed-effects specification:

```
speed_{i,t} = alpha_i + gamma_t + beta * (treated_i * post_t) + eps_{i,t}
```

- `alpha_i`: link fixed effects.
- `gamma_t`: time fixed effects (date-by-hour, or day + hour-of-week).
- `beta`: average treatment effect on the treated (ATT), in mph.
- Standard errors clustered by `link_id`; two-way (link and date) as a
  robustness check.
- Unit of observation: link × hour. Estimated on the full panel and,
  separately, on peak-only / off-peak / weekday / weekend subsets.

### Identifying assumptions

- **Parallel trends**: absent treatment, treated and control links would have
  evolved similarly. Assessed via pre-period event-study coefficients — not
  assumed proven.
- **No anticipation**: behaviour does not change before 2025-01-05. Tested with
  leads.
- **SUTVA / spillovers**: diversion to boundary links violates SUTVA; handled by
  (a) excluding boundary links from controls and (b) a separate spillover
  regression on boundary links.
- **Stable measurement**: the probe panel and link definitions do not change
  discontinuously at the treatment date. Checked in the data-quality report.

### What beta does and does not imply

`beta` is the average change in hourly median speed on treated links,
differenced against control links, over the post period. It is **not** a
statement about any single corridor, about volume, about travel time
door-to-door, or about welfare. Practical significance is judged separately
from statistical significance (mph change relative to baseline speed).

## Design 3 — Event study (Phase 8)

```
speed_{i,t} = alpha_i + gamma_t + sum_{k != -1} theta_k * 1[event_time_{i,t} = k] + eps_{i,t}
```

- `event_time` in weeks relative to treatment (months as a robustness check).
- Reference period `k = -1`.
- Plot `theta_k` with 95% CIs: flat, near-zero pre-period coefficients support
  parallel trends; the post-period path shows the dynamic adjustment.
- If pre-period coefficients are jointly significant or trending, the parallel-
  trends assumption is not credible and causal language is withheld.
- Sun & Abraham (2021) / Callaway & Sant'Anna (2021) only if treatment timing
  effectively varies (e.g. staged enforcement) — not expected here.

## Robustness (Phase 9 — separate specifications, not edits to the primary)

- Alternative control groups (outer-borough only; matched on pre-treatment
  speed; synthetic control).
- Controls farther from the toll boundary.
- Alternative pre/post windows; drop the ±2-week transition around 2025-01-05.
- Placebo treatment dates in the pre-period.
- Peak vs off-peak; weekday vs weekend.
- Exclude poor-coverage links and known outage periods.
- Alternative aggregation (daily; link-week).
- Weather controls in/out (added only if descriptive analysis shows imbalance).

Each produces a row in a comparison table showing whether the conclusion
materially changes.
